How to label

If a quad is already drawn (from a loaded guess), drag its 4 corners into place, or confirm it's already correct by just moving on.

If no quad exists, click 4 points on the card's corners in order: top-left, top-right, bottom-right, bottom-left.

Shortcuts

→ / Space next frame
← previous frame
N mark "no card" / clear
Backspace undo last point (while placing)
